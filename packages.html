Why pricing is project-specific

Lot conditions, existing documentation, jurisdiction rules, project size, structural scope, consultant needs, and number of review cycles can materially change the work. We review the basic facts before presenting a written scope and fee.

Every proposal should tell you

  • What Forma will produce
  • How many design or revision rounds are included
  • Which outside consultants are expected
  • Which agency and third-party fees are separate
  • What information the owner must provide
  • How additional services are approved
