California design + permitting

A process you can see.

Every project is different, but the work should never feel mysterious. We use defined milestones, decisions, and deliverables from discovery through permit review.

Discover

Confirm the property, goals, desired use, decision-makers, known constraints, and the information available.

Define

Set the service scope, deliverables, outside consultants, assumptions, schedule framework, and fee structure.

Design

Develop the site relationship, layout, form, and key selections through reviewable design checkpoints.

Document

Prepare the agreed drawing package and coordinate structural, energy, or other technical inputs.

Submit + respond

Organize the submittal and track agency comments, owner decisions, and drawing revisions within scope.

Hand off

Issue the approved or final documents and support an organized transition to contractor pricing and construction.

Communication at every milestone

Our proposals identify what is included, what is excluded, and what information we need from you. If the project changes, we explain the impact before expanding the work.

Make the first move.

Tell us the property location, project type, and what you want the space to accomplish. We’ll review the basics and outline the right next step.
