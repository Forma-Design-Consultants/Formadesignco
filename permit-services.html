California design + permitting

A clearer permit path.

We organize project documents, outside consultants, submittal requirements, and review comments so the process is easier to understand and act on.

Permitting is a review process, not a one-click approval. The best way to reduce avoidable delay is to submit a coordinated package and respond clearly when reviewers ask for changes.

How Forma supports permitting

  • Jurisdiction and submittal-requirement research
  • Drawing and consultant-package coordination
  • Digital submission organization when included in scope
  • Plan-check comment review and response tracking
  • Revisions to Forma drawings within the agreed scope
  • Communication checkpoints with the owner and project team

What stays transparent

Agency fees, review timing, approvals, third-party consultant fees, and construction costs are controlled by others and cannot be guaranteed. Forma defines its services and assumptions in the project proposal so responsibilities are clear.

Can you guarantee permit approval?

No consultant can responsibly guarantee an agency decision. We can prepare and coordinate the agreed documents, address review comments, and keep responsibilities visible.

Are city fees included?

Jurisdiction fees and assessments are typically paid directly by the owner and are separate unless a proposal explicitly says otherwise.

How long does review take?

Timing depends on the jurisdiction, project complexity, submission completeness, and review cycles. We provide project-specific expectations after initial discovery.
